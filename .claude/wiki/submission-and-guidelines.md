# Submission & Guidelines

## How to participate

- Takes place on the **lablab.ai platform** and the **lablab.ai Discord server** — register for both.
- Click **Enroll** on the hackathon page, then read: Hackathon Guidelines, Getting Started Guide, and the lablab.ai Hackathon Rule Book.
- Open to everyone regardless of prior AI/coding experience.
- Before kickoff: browse AI Tech and tutorials pages to get a head start on the available technologies.

## Teams

- Team size: **1–6 people**.
- No team yet → use the dashboard or Discord to find teammates and bounce ideas.

## What to submit

**Basic information**
- Project title
- Short description
- Long description
- Technology & category tags

**Cover image and presentation**
- Cover image
- Video presentation
- Slide presentation

**App hosting and repository**
- Public GitHub repository
- Demo application platform / Application URL
- **Alpaca paper trading account ID** — required for judging (lets judges pull actual trading activity and P&L; see [challenge-and-requirements.md](challenge-and-requirements.md))

**Social engagement (optional, up to 5)**
- Up to 5 links to X/LinkedIn posts made during the hackathon, tagging both lablab.ai and Alpaca.
