## Judging criteria

1. **P&L Performance** — actual trading performance of the submitted agent in the Alpaca paper trading environment; judges weigh both the P&L number and how effectively the strategy performs through its trading activity.
2. **Technology Implementation** — how effectively the project uses Alpaca's Trading API, MCP server, CLI, and other required technologies to build an autonomous trading agent.
3. **Creativity & Originality** — originality of concept, trading strategy, agent behavior, and overall approach.
4. **Presentation & Execution** — clarity of communication, demonstration of the agent in action, and quality of the reasoning behind strategy and results.
5. **Social engagement** (separate track, see above) — content quality + generated engagement.

**Implication:** P&L is listed first and is judged directly off the paper account's trade history (not self-reported) — the account ID requirement exists precisely so judges can verify this independently. A compelling write-up cannot substitute for a verifiably profitable (or well-reasoned, risk-managed) trading record.

## Prize terms

- **Sponsor:** AlpacaDB, Inc. pays the $6,000 base pool directly in USD.
- **Individual payee:** prizes are paid to individuals, not teams/companies. A winning team must designate one member to receive the full amount (or arrange a split with Alpaca Finance in advance).
- **Payment timing:** within 90 days of event end, once required documents clear (including international sanctions screening).
- **Required documents:** W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (non-US), government photo ID, bank details.
- **Taxes:** US winners receiving >$600 get a 1099-MISC. Non-US payments generally subject to 30% US withholding unless a valid tax-treaty claim applies on the W-8BEN. Gross prizes may be reduced by withholding and wire fees.
- **Deadline risk:** winners must complete documentation within 90 days of winner notification or the prize may be **forfeited**.
- **Eligibility:** 18+; not available to Alpaca employees, contractors, immediate family/household members, or participants from sanctioned countries. No purchase or Alpaca account required to enter (but an account is required to actually build/submit).
- Judging is final (skill contest). Submissions must be original and MIT-license-compliant.
- Alpaca may use winner name/likeness/project for publicity without additional compensation, and may modify/cancel prizes if the event changes or is cancelled.
